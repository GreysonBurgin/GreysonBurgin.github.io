Welcome

In August of 2026 I climbed Mount Kilimanjaro with my mom on a nine-day trip with Thompson Treks. We took the Western approach, the Lemosho Route, which starts on the far side of the mountain and takes longer than most routes. The extra days give your body time to adjust to the altitude before summit day.

It took close to seventy people to get the eleven of us up the mountain. Guides, chefs, and porters carried the food, the tents, the extra water, and our duffels for the entire week. The summit photo every climber takes is built to leave most of them out. This site is for both sides of that story.
