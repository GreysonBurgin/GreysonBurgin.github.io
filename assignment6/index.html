Restless Mind

Sam Barber's debut album, and an album I will forever come back to.

About the album

An amazing mix of excellent vocals and emotion with real truths baked in. The world-famous classic from this album is Indigo, and if you haven't listened to Sam Barber before, it's not a bad place to start.
